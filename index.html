Line

Pen 1 draws the outlines, pen 2 the tone lines, pen 3 the hatching. Heavy outlines go over each one three times; load a thicker pen 1 to push the lineweights further.

Pen 1 is the dark ink for the shadows, pen 2 the light ink for highlights and midtones. Where they overlap you get the mix. Pick the inks under Pen colours.

Outlines go on pen 1 and the fill on pen 2, so you can plot them in two colours.

Cell fill

Outlines stay on pen 1 and the fill goes on pen 2 (pen 3 for Subdiv), so you can plot it in two colours. Darker cells get tighter fills, and Cross, Grid and Iso add directions as the tone deepens.

Background

Colour bands

One pen per band, pens 1 to N from the start of the gradient (set the inks under Pen colours). Edge blend scatters strokes across each boundary so some marks take the neighbouring pen. Radial bands centre on the focal point when you have placed one. This replaces the style's own pen layers.

Pen colours

Duotone always uses pens 1 and 2. Each pen is its own layer in the SVG, coloured to match, so you can swap pens between layers.

Focal point

Long-press the picture to place a point the style reacts to. Keep holding and drag to move it.

Paper

Export

Every SVG keeps each pen on its own coloured layer. Turn on the option above for plotter software that ignores layers: you get one file per colour. Send SVG opens your phone's share sheet, so you can AirDrop, Nearby Share or email the files straight to the plotter computer.

Get the app
